## When to Use Each System

### Use Single-Agent (Fast Screening) 🏃

**Best for:**
- ✅ Initial screening of large company universe (100+ companies)
- ✅ Internal risk management dashboards
- ✅ Quick financial health checks
- ✅ Time-sensitive decisions (<30 seconds required)
- ✅ Pure financial analysis (no competitive context needed)

**Example Workflow:**
```
Screen 100 companies → Single-agent (50 minutes)
  ↓
Identify 20 candidates with financial health >60/100
  ↓
Deep dive on 20 candidates → Multi-agent (30 minutes)
  ↓
Final recommendation: 5 BUY, 10 HOLD, 5 SELL
```

---

### Use Multi-Agent (Investment Decisions) 🎯

**Best for:**
- ✅ Buy/hold/sell investment decisions
- ✅ Investment committee presentations
- ✅ Client-facing research reports
- ✅ Comprehensive due diligence
- ✅ Competitive/strategic assessment required
- ✅ Event-driven investing (need catalysts + timing)

**Example Workflow:**
```
Due diligence on acquisition target → Multi-agent (80 seconds)
  ↓
Comprehensive report with:
  - Competitive positioning (42/100 - Laggard)
  - Strategic assessment (73/100 - Adequate)
  - Forward-looking catalysts (green projects, commodity risks)
  - Cross-perspective synthesis (liquidity + competitive + strategic weaknesses converge)
  - Decision framework (specific triggers, monitoring plan)
  ↓
Present to investment committee → Immediate decision
```

## Final Recommendation

### Two-Stage Workflow (Optimal)

**Stage 1: Screening (Single-Agent)**
- Run on all companies in universe
- Filter for basic financial health + risk thresholds
- Time: ~30 seconds per company
- Output: Screened candidate list

**Stage 2: Deep Dive (Multi-Agent)**
- Run on screened candidates
- Generate investment recommendation with decision framework
- Time: ~80 seconds per company
- Output: Buy/hold/sell with monitoring plan

**Total Time for 100-Company Universe:**
- Single-agent screening: 100 × 30s = 50 minutes
- Multi-agent deep dive (20 finalists): 20 × 80s = 27 minutes
- **Total: 77 minutes for comprehensive coverage**

**vs Single-Stage Multi-Agent:**
- Multi-agent on all 100: 100 × 80s = 133 minutes

**Efficiency Gain:** 42% time savings with same quality on finalists
