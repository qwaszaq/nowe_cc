# Report Quality Comparison: Single-Agent vs Multi-Agent Intelligence Systems

**Date:** 2025-11-07
**Test Data:** Grupa Azoty S.A. 2024 (Chemicals & Fertilizers)
**Comparison:** Single-Agent (4-pass local LLM) vs Multi-Agent (6 specialized agents)

---

## Executive Summary

This document provides a detailed quality assessment comparing intelligence reports generated by two different architectures using the same financial data. The multi-agent system demonstrates **superior structure, actionability, and professional formatting**, while the single-agent system produces **more verbose analysis but lacks clear organization**.

**Key Finding:** Multi-agent reports are significantly more suitable for professional investment analysis workflows due to clear structure, explicit scoring, and actionable recommendations.

---

## Methodology

### Evaluation Criteria

Reports evaluated across 8 dimensions:

1. **Structure & Organization** - Hierarchy, sections, navigation
2. **Executive Summary Quality** - Clarity, completeness, actionability
3. **Analytical Depth** - Thoroughness, insights, reasoning
4. **Scoring & Metrics** - Quantitative assessments, transparency
5. **Recommendations** - Clarity, rationale, actionability
6. **Source Attribution** - Citations, traceability
7. **Professional Format** - Investment memo standards
8. **Readability** - Clarity, conciseness, audience fit

### Scoring Scale

- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Excellent (90-100%) - Professional grade
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Good (75-89%) - Strong with minor gaps
- ⭐⭐⭐ Adequate (60-74%) - Acceptable but improvable
- ⭐⭐ Weak (40-59%) - Significant issues
- ⭐ Poor (0-39%) - Major deficiencies
