# Single-Agent vs Multi-Agent Intelligence System Comparison

**Analysis Date:** November 6, 2025
**Company:** Grupa Azoty S.A.
**Industry:** Chemicals & Fertilizers
**Comparison:** Single-Agent (Priority 1) vs Multi-Agent (Priority 4)

---

## Executive Summary

This document compares two intelligence analysis approaches for Grupa Azoty S.A.:

**Single-Agent System** (Priority 1)
- 2 specialized analyses: Financial Health + Risk Assessment
- Generation time: 26.57 seconds
- Report length: 20,579 characters
- RAG: Not enabled in this test

**Multi-Agent System** (Priority 4)
- 6 specialized agents: Financial + Risk + Industry + Strategy + Market + Synthesis
- Generation time: 80.32 seconds
- Report length: 10,067 characters
- RAG: Fully enabled with section-filtered retrieval

### Key Finding

The **multi-agent system provides superior investment decision support** despite being more concise. It delivers:
- ✅ **Competitive context** (missing from single-agent)
- ✅ **Strategic evaluation** (missing from single-agent)
- ✅ **Forward-looking catalysts** (missing from single-agent)
- ✅ **Cross-perspective synthesis** with conflict resolution
- ✅ **Bull/bear/base case scenarios** with probability distribution
- ✅ **Specific decision triggers** (quantified upgrade/downgrade conditions)

## Overall Comparison Summary

### Strengths: Single-Agent System

✅ **Faster:** 26.57s vs 80.32s (3x faster)
✅ **Detailed risk analysis:** Comprehensive risk matrices with mitigation
✅ **Good for quick financial screening:** If you only need financial health + risk, it's efficient
✅ **Longer format:** More verbose explanations (20k chars vs 10k chars)

### Weaknesses: Single-Agent System

❌ **No competitive context:** Cannot assess market position or industry dynamics
❌ **No strategic evaluation:** Cannot assess management quality or strategy credibility
❌ **No forward-looking analysis:** Lacks catalysts, timing, or event-driven insights
❌ **No cross-perspective synthesis:** Potential conflicts not identified or resolved
❌ **No probability-weighted scenarios:** Bull/bear cases lack probability distribution
❌ **No monitoring framework:** No guidance on what to track or how frequently
❌ **No document grounding:** Generic claims without company-specific evidence

### Strengths: Multi-Agent System

✅ **Comprehensive coverage:** 6 perspectives vs 2
✅ **Competitive & industry context:** Critical for relative valuation
✅ **Strategic evaluation:** Assesses management quality, strategy, capital allocation
✅ **Forward-looking analysis:** Catalysts with timing and probability
✅ **Cross-perspective synthesis:** Converging/conflicting signals explicitly resolved
✅ **Probability-weighted scenarios:** 25%/55%/20% bull/bear/base distribution

❌ **Slower:** 80.32s vs 26.57s (3x slower)
❌ **More concise:** Less verbose (could be seen as less detailed)
❌ **More complex:** 6 agents vs 2 passes (harder to debug/optimize)

## Use Case Recommendations

### Use Single-Agent When:

✅ **Quick screening needed** - Need rapid financial health + risk assessment
✅ **Time-sensitive** - Must generate report in <30 seconds
✅ **Financial-only focus** - Investment decision based purely on financials, not competitive position
✅ **Internal use** - For internal risk management, not external investment recommendation

### Use Multi-Agent When:

✅ **Investment decision-making** - Need comprehensive buy/hold/sell recommendation
✅ **Competitive analysis required** - Must understand market position and industry dynamics
✅ **Strategic assessment needed** - Evaluating management quality, strategy credibility
✅ **Event-driven investing** - Need catalysts and timing for entry/exit
✅ **External reporting** - Presenting to investment committee or clients
✅ **Comprehensive due diligence** - Full 360-degree company analysis

**For investors making buy/hold/sell decisions**, the multi-agent system is **decisively superior**:
- Provides competitive context (is this a leader or laggard?)

### Recommendation

**Deploy both systems in a two-stage workflow:**

**Stage 1: Screening (Single-Agent)**
- Run single-agent analysis on all companies in universe
- Filter for companies meeting basic financial/risk thresholds
- Time per company: ~30 seconds
- Output: Screened list of candidates

**Stage 2: Deep Dive (Multi-Agent)**
- Run multi-agent analysis on screened candidates
- Generate comprehensive investment recommendation
- Time per company: ~80 seconds
- Output: Buy/hold/sell recommendation with decision framework

**Total efficiency gain:** Screen 100 companies in 50 minutes (single-agent), then deep-dive on 10-20 finalists in 15-30 minutes (multi-agent) = comprehensive coverage in <90 minutes.
